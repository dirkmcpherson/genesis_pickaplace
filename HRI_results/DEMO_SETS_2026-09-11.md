## 1. Provenance

### Human set — `dHfull_all` (74 tapes)

- **Origin.** Teleoperated Kinova gen3-lite pick → place → slide trials, all from ONE afternoon
  (the 2024-12-18 session). Participant identity is unknown and unrecorded — one person and
  several people are equally consistent with the data (`paper/E2E_AUDIT_BRIEF_2026-09-10.md` §11).
  A further 120 trials from three earlier days were recovered on 2026-09-08 and are a disclosed
  separate pool, NOT in this set.
- **Sim form.** Each trial re-executed in the world of record `gc_kp4_riser3_shelf6` (`action_repeat
  4`, `delta_joint`, `delta_cap 0.025`, 17-dim state), recording lineage `dHfull_w3` (NOT the
  bit-exact census lineage: action streams differ on 24/74, `paper/SLIDE_CLAUSE5_LINEAGE_2026-09-07.md`
  §7). Manifest `$W/demos_state_full/dHfull_all/repeat.json`, `src` sha `3b478c9a…`.
- **Selection: none.** Every attempt kept, including the 10 tapes in which the human never picked
  (uids 234, 278, 286, 293, 294, 295, 300, 301, 318, 319; 234 and 318 are the lying-can starts of
  CONFOUNDS row 51, unwinnable by construction).
- **Reward column.** Relabelled by re-execution under the unified ladder (`_rz` staged, `_rs`
  sparse; action streams byte-identical to the source on 74/74, `paper/LADDER_PILOT_LOG_2026-09-11.md`).
- **Shape.** Decisions per tape min/median/max 2 / 389 / 601; idle fraction 0.365.

### Machine set — `dDPfull_first` (72 tapes)

- **Teacher.** A Diffusion Policy, `dp_phase/dHfull_pruned_DP_s0` (100k steps, lerobot), trained
  on `matched_w3/dHfull_pruned`. Verified 2026-09-11 from the artefacts: its
  `checkpoints/100000/pretrained_model/train_config.json` reads
  `dataset.root = baselines/matched_w3/dHfull_pruned/lerobot`.
- **What `dHfull_pruned` is.** The SAME 74 human recordings minus the 10 no-pick tapes (64), with
  idle runs collapsed ONLY in the segment before the grasp (`prune_full_v1.py`: margin 38
  decisions ≈ 5 s before the pick, `idle_eps 1e-3` over all 7 action channels, grip included).
  Everything from 5 s before the pick through the place and the slide is untouched — the last 50
  actions of all 64 tapes are bit-identical to the raw tapes (Lane 8, amendment (ab)). It is a
  **full-task** set; "pruned" refers to pre-pick idle time, not to scope.
- **Harvest.** 195 rollouts on the human starts (`$W/demos_state_full/src_dDPfull`, up to 3
  attempts per start, `record_demos --verify`); every one of the 195 stamps
  `teacher_ckpt = …/dp_phase/dHfull_pruned_DP_s0/checkpoints/100000/pretrained_model`.
- **Selection: FIRST attempt per start → 72 tapes** (`one_per_ic_first=True`, PHASE_PLAN (v)).
  The earlier best-of-3 set `dDPfull` (Σ reward 206 v 131 de-selected; `best ≠ first` on 24/72
  starts) is retired for comparisons because selection, not source, produced its advantage.
  Manifest `$W/demos_state_full/dDPfull_first/repeat.json`, `src` sha `db95084f…`.
- **Shape.** Decisions min/median/max 2 / 601 / 601 (the cap); idle fraction 0.007.

### What the contrast therefore is

Raw human demonstrations versus demonstrations produced by an imitator trained on the pruned,
no-pick-free version of those same demonstrations. The arms differ in source AND in two pruning
effects (idle collapse; the 10 no-pick tapes absent from the teacher's data). {RLPD} and
{r2dreamer} were indifferent to pruning in pick scope (`paper/RESULTS_WM_HUMAN_VS_MACHINE_2026-09-04.md`,
MORNING_TABLE §5); {DP} was not (+0.19 to +0.27). State this as a property of the machine set.

## 2. Phase-scoring method (the "re-execution census")

A demonstration is scored by **replaying its recorded action stream through the training
environment itself** — `FullTaskEnv(scope='full')`, world `gc_kp4_riser3_shelf6`, the same code
path a training run uses — and reading the stage flags the environment grants. Since Ladder N
(2026-09-11) the scoring is done ONCE per tape with no termination, every frame's predicates are
written to a stage record (`$W/stage_records_2026-09-11/<set>/`), and every reward ladder is
applied OFFLINE to those records by the same accountant object the env runs
(`baselines/rl/relabel_reward.py --from-records`; verified offline == direct on 146/146 tapes).
So every set below shares one scoring pass; the ladders differ only in what they pay. Flags are
sticky: a tape "reaches" a stage if the flag fires at any decision.

Predicates in use (`baselines/stage_predicates.py`, `baselines/rl/full_env.py`; stamp
`unified-2026-09-10`, `genesis_can_env=40544bf73c8c`):

| stage | definition |
|---|---|
| `picked` | the env's hardened held-can flag |
| `placed_v2` | grip cmd < 0.45 ∧ can in the shelf footprint ∧ z in the resting band ∧ tilt < 20°, sustained 10 env frames |
| `farside` | after a set-down (release latch), the tool reaches the far side of the can: within 0.10 m of the can and inside a 60° cone about the can→goal line |
| `slide_event` | `farside` ∧ the can then moves ≥ 1 cm goalward while not in hand |
| `nested_v2` | picked ∧ `placed_v2` ∧ dist_xy(can, goal) ≤ 0.081 m ∧ both cans tilt < 20° ∧ z band ∧ not in hand (tool–can lever ≥ 2.5 cm) ∧ at rest (≤ 2 mm over 12 frames) |
| **`home`** | `slide_event` ∧ `nested_v2` — **the task outcome and the only paid event under the sparse ladders**; a drop into the goal cannot score it |
| `tipped` | can tilt > 60° ∧ not in hand, sustained 4 env frames — terminal, penalty 0 (`tip_guard=not_in_hand`, `paper/TIP_RULE_2026-09-11.md`) |

Validation: `nested_v2` v the post-episode settle 60/60 on policy episodes (precision 1.00 /
recall 1.00; `paper/NESTED_V2_PREDICATE_2026-09-10.md`); `home` fires on 13/13 human and 14/14
machine sim-slides with 0 false positives on the demonstration set
(`paper/LADDER_N_DEMO_CHECK_2026-09-11.md`).

**Hardware caveat.** Re-execution is class-sensitive after contact. The state sets of record were
re-executed on a 64-core AVX-512 node (pax146, job 3575953); the pixel sets had to be re-executed
on the 32-core AVX2 build box (renders), and differ on 1 human / 2 machine `home` tapes (§3).
Disclosed, not corrected.

## 4. Reward ladders in use

Applied offline to the stage records; each rung paid once per episode; `max_return` and the
world model's `return_clamp` are derived from the registry (`full_env.LADDERS`). Every ladder
change is a retrain.

| ladder | rungs | terminal | max | status (2026-09-14) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `nested_sparse` | `home` +1 | home, tipped | 1 | **primary for the state-based world-model arm** (human ignited 3/4 v machine 0/4 at 4M; +4 v +4 running) |
| `nested_sparse10` | `home` +10 | home, tipped | 10 | **primary for the pixel arms** (all learners); state-based 2 v 2 dead (amendment (ac), disconfirmed) |
| `nested_ramp` | picked +1, placed_v2 +1, +3·min(1, slide/0.05 m) after `farside`, home +4 | home, tipped | 9 | 8 v 8 world-model comparison completing; ramp saturates at 5 cm (slides without nesting) |
| `staged` | picked +1, placed_v2 +1, contact_push +2, slide_success +4 | slide_success, tipped | 8 | control only (pre-Ladder-N; its rungs use retired predicates) |

{Diffusion Policy} ignores reward; its cells terminate under `nested_sparse10` rules so its
`home` counts end where the other learners' do. Every learner's statistic of record is the
per-seed rnd30-MODE `home` count (30 random starts), with ignition = ≥ 1 `home`.
