# End-to-end (full-task) experiment: complete write-up of the world-model arm

Status 2026-09-08. **The world-model arm is complete and corrected.** Diffusion Policy and RLPD end-to-end runs are in
training (§8). Numbers below are from the pinned re-score (`fresh_eval_*_v2`), every cell stamped with bank, entry-pinning
and hardware provenance. Registration: `PHASE_PLAN_2026-09-04.md` amendment (d), plus (j) and (l).

## 1. What the experiment is

From the pick-scope initial conditions (can on the table, arm at home), learn the whole task under the staged sparse
reward — picked 1 / placed 1 / contact 2 / nested 4, each paid once, `nested` terminating — for 2e6 simulator steps,
r2dreamer recipe of record, **8 seeds per arm**.

| arm | set | tapes | content |
|---|---|---|---|
| human | `dHfull_all` | 74 | **every** full-task tape of the 74 success uids, including 10 no-picks and 43 pick-only |
| machine | `dDPfull` | 72 | the 195-tape DP harvest reduced to **the best attempt per IC** (highest recorded reward) |

## 4. Power is the real limit on every claim here

At 8 v 8 with this seed spread, **most stages cannot detect an effect the size of the margin they are tested against**:

| MDE below the ±0.10 margin | MDE above it |
|---|---|
| slide_success 0.053, nested_honest 0.088 (rnd30 MODE) | picked 0.210, contact 0.243, contact_push 0.169, placed_v2 0.154, nested_proxy 0.141 |

So the defensible sentence is **"no source effect detectable at this sample size"**, never "the arms are equivalent".
Only the two adequately powered stages support anything stronger, and both are the stages that matter (the completion
predicate and the statistic of record).

## 5. The one consistent signal — and it favours the machine arm

`nested_honest` is negative in **all three** cells (−0.058 rnd30 MODE, −0.017 rnd30 SAMPLE, −0.067 hold15 MODE), and
`placed_v2` is negative in all three as well (−0.038, −0.062, −0.158). Nothing reaches significance, but **every hint runs
the same way: the machine arm completes the task honestly more often** — 0.104 v 0.046 on the statistic of record, a
2.3× ratio where the published proxy showed only 1.4×.

## 6. What the corrections changed, and why the published numbers move

1. **`nested` was the training proxy, not completion.** The published 0.163/0.192 is sticky-contact ∧ grip commanded open
   ∧ both upright, granted without proximity or settle. Honest settled completion is **0.046/0.104** — the published
   figure over-counts ~3× (human) and ~1.8× (machine).
2. **`placed` was unearnable** (stale base-world band, 0/240 both arms). `placed_v2` replaces it and **is** earnable
   (0.162/0.200) — which **refutes by measurement** the published "policies carry the can in without ever releasing"
   reading: they do release.
3. **The published cells mixed hardware classes across arms** — the human arm had 2 of 8 seeds on a 36-core AVX2 class,
   the machine arm none, and cross-class movement reaches 0.100 on `contact`. All 64 re-scored cells are pinned to one
   class (Xeon Gold 6438M, verifiable per cell).
4. Learning curves were rebuilt after a truncation defect (§9 of `curves/README.md`).

## 7. Caveats that limit interpretation

- **`slide_success` scores a withdrawn predicate.** It implements (l) (`grip < 0.3`), which **(p) withdrew** — that clause
  passes only **2 of 74 demonstrations**, because the demonstrated slide is "release fully, re-close to ≈ 0.4, push home".
  Treat the column as a diagnostic until (p) clause 5 is calibrated.
- **The arms are matched on tape count, not content.** Machine Σ reward 206 v human 118; 16 v 3 demonstrated nested
  completions; the machine set is **best-of-3 per IC** while the human set keeps every attempt including failures. Any
  machine-favouring result — which is the direction of every hint here — is equally consistent with *how the set was
  built*.
- **Human demos carry 36.5 % idle decisions** (39.3 % pre-pick) against 0.7 % machine.
- **`hold15` is not held out** (14/15 training starts); read it as in-distribution.
- **Reproducibility is conditional**: an end-to-end cell reproduces only re-run **as a whole 30-episode sequence on the
  same CPU class**. Within-episode the policy samples its latent from global RNG, so single episodes pulled out of a
  sequence do not reproduce.

## 8. Learning speed — the one place the human arm leads

From training rollouts (world model only; RLPD/DP cannot produce these — see `curves/README.md`), ignition = first binned
step reaching 0.2, 8 v 8 permutation on per-seed steps:

| stage | human | machine | Δ | p |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| picked | 424,998 | 574,998 | **−156,249** | **0.019** |
| contact | 824,997 | 949,996 | −187,499 | **0.044** |
| nested_proxy | 1,024,996 | 1,224,995 | −249,999 | 0.059 |

The human arm reaches each stage earlier while ending level or behind. **Flat on pick, place and slide** (p 0.505, 0.414,
1.000) — the effect is specific to the long-horizon task, which is what makes it a hypothesis rather than an artefact.
Exploratory: thresholds and grid chosen after seeing the data, one learner, and neither arm has converged at 2e6 (both
still improving, machine ~2× faster — see `PROPOSAL_longer_runs_2026-09-08.md`).

## 10. What can and cannot be said today

**Can:** on the full task from scratch, with 8 seeds per arm and the corrected predicates, **no demonstration-source
effect is detectable at any stage on the statistic of record**; the honest completion rate is ~0.05 (human) and ~0.10
(machine), far below the proxy previously published; and the human arm reaches each stage measurably sooner while ending
no better.

**Cannot:** that the arms are *equivalent* (five of seven stages are underpowered against the margin); that the machine
arm is better (no comparison reaches significance, and the one P2 breach is an underpowered in-distribution cell); or
that any of this is a *source* effect rather than a *set-construction* effect, while the machine set remains best-of-3
and the human set unfiltered.

## 11. Removing the best-of-3 confound — measured, and it needs no new data

The 195-tape harvest still exists (`demos_v2/dDPfull_w3{,_partial,_fails}`): **72 ICs, 64 of them with more than one
attempt**, and the set of record was built by keeping the highest-reward attempt per IC. Reconstructing attempt order from
the sequential rollout uids, here is exactly what that selection bought:

| machine set | Σ reward | mean | nested demos | picked |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **BEST per IC** (the set of record) | **206** | 2.86 | **16** | 70 |
| **FIRST attempt per IC** | **131** | 1.82 | **8** | 63 |
| RANDOM attempt (seeds 0/1/2) | 139 / 159 / 150 | ~2.1 | 9 / 12 / 10 | 67 / 65 / 68 |
| **HUMAN** `dHfull_all` (one attempt, no selection) | **118** | 1.59 | **3** | 64 |

**The selection is doing most of the work.** Best-of-3 raises total reward **131 → 206 (+57 %)** and *doubles* the
demonstrated completions **8 → 16**; `best ≠ first` on **24 of 72** ICs. Against the human set the difference collapses
once selection is removed: Σ reward 131 v 118 (+11 %, against +75 % as selected) and **picked 63 v 64 — indistinguishable**.
So the machine set's apparent superiority as *training data* is substantially an artefact of how we chose the tapes.

**The fix, and why it is the only symmetric one.** Selection cannot be matched *upward*: each human uid has exactly one
recorded attempt, and re-executing it in a deterministic sim reproduces the same tape, so there is no human "best of 3" to
construct. The asymmetry can therefore only be removed by **de-selecting the machine arm**:

1. Rebuild the machine set as **FIRST-attempt-per-IC** from the existing 195 tapes — same protocol as the human arm ("one
   attempt per start, keep whatever happened"). No new harvesting; the data is on disk.
2. Retrain the end-to-end machine arm on it (8 seeds, or 4 as a pilot ≈ 40 GPU-h).
3. Two contrasts then fall out: **human v machine-first** is the de-confounded source comparison, and **machine-best v
   machine-first** isolates the selection effect on its own.

**Why this matters for the one positive result.** The learning-speed effect (human reaches each stage ~156k steps sooner)
is currently measured against the *selected* machine set — the arm with twice the completions and 57 % more reward. Its
direction is already counterintuitive on those terms (better demonstrations, slower ignition), so de-selection is
diagnostic either way: if the human lead grows, the effect is not about demonstration quality; if it shrinks, the effect
was partly our filtering. Until that is run, a significant learning-speed result could not be attributed to demonstration
*source* rather than to set *construction*.

### 11.1 Executed — registered, built and training (2026-09-08)

Registered first as **PHASE_PLAN amendment (v)** (commit `6d1915b`, before the build job existed), then built and
submitted. The three registered predictions are in the amendment; the decisive one is **P3**, whose three readings —
the human ignition advantage persists, shrinks toward zero, or reverses — are fixed in advance, and none of which is
treated as confirming a source effect on its own, because the sets still differ in idle fraction and in whether failures
are included.

**The set.** `--one-per-ic-first` (new flag, commit `364e65c`): one tape per start, chosen by the **lowest rollout uid**
= the first attempt, with **no reward term**. Built by the same converter, the same source symlink tree and the same
selector code path as the set of record — only the selection rule differs. The build asserted the numbers registered in
(v) *before* it wrote anything and passed: 72 starts, Σ 131, completions 8, picked 63.

| | Σ reward | picked | contact | completions | decisions | idle frac |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| machine, BEST per start (of record) | 206 | 70 | 36 | 16 | 32 851 | 0.007 |
| **machine, FIRST attempt** | **131** | **63** | **18** | **8** | 36 834 | 0.009 |
| human, every attempt | 118 | 64 | 21 | 3 | 29 221 | 0.365 |

De-selection does not merely shrink the machine arm's advantage — on **picked (63 v 64)** and **contact (18 v 21)** it
puts the machine set *below* the human set, leaving it ahead only on completions (8 v 3). The remaining large asymmetry
is the **idle fraction**, which de-selection does not touch and which no version of this comparison controls.

Built in all three learners' formats and cross-checked row-for-row against the source tapes (`CHECK-OK`, sha
`ebb7bd84`); 2 one-decision tapes fall under the lerobot minimum, so Diffusion Policy trains on 70 of 72 while the world
model and RLPD train on all 72 — the same disclosure the sets of record carry.

**Runs.** 4 seeds × 3 learners = 12: `v1st_wm_s0-3`, `v1st_rlpd_s0-3`, `v1st_dp_s0-3`, at the budgets of their
respective sets of record (world model 2e6 sim steps, RLPD 250k decisions, DP 100k gradient steps). Both downstream
launchers were given the new arm and a gate that **positively asserts** it is training on the first-attempt set, so
neither can silently train on the selected one.

**Reading it out.** `e2e_table_all.py` now takes a name template per arm per learner (defaults unchanged, and verified
to reproduce §5.1 exactly: rnd30 mode picked 0.500 v 0.537, contact 0.379 v 0.338). Any non-default arm prints a banner
saying the table is **not** the one of record, so a de-confounded cell cannot be mistaken for a published one. Two
contrasts, both from the same command:

```bash
cd $LAB/gp_e2e
# A -- de-confounded SOURCE: human v machine-first
python3 baselines/e2e_table_all.py --seeds 0-3 --sets rnd30 spots60 \
  --wm-machine 'full_r2d_state_dDPfull_first_bnormclampS8ent5_s{s}' \
  --rlpd-machine 'e2e_rlpd_dDPfirst_s{s}' --dp-machine 'e2e_dp_dDPfirst_s{s}'
# B -- SELECTION alone: machine-best v machine-first (identical source, one filtered)
python3 baselines/e2e_table_all.py --seeds 0-3 --sets rnd30 spots60 \
  --wm-human 'full_r2d_state_dDPfull_bnormclampS8ent5_s{s}' --wm-machine 'full_r2d_state_dDPfull_first_bnormclampS8ent5_s{s}' \
  --rlpd-human 'e2e_rlpd_dDP_s{s}' --rlpd-machine 'e2e_rlpd_dDPfirst_s{s}' \
  --dp-human 'e2e_dp_dDP_s{s}' --dp-machine 'e2e_dp_dDPfirst_s{s}'
```

Contrast B is the one with no confound left in it at all: **the same generator, the same starts, the same budgets — the
only difference is whether we kept the best of three attempts or the first.** Whatever separates those two arms is
selection, and nothing else. Note both read 4 seeds against the record's 8, so their MDEs will be wider; the comparison
of record stays the 8-seed machine-best arm until (v) is extended.
