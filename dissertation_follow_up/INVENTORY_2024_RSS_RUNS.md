# Inventory: the 2024 PinPad-four and PushT runs and demonstration sets (surveyed 2026-09-17)

Everything below is on the **pop-os workstation**, under `~/workspace/fastrl/RSS_logs/`. Nothing is on the cluster.
Counts come from reading the folders and each run's `metrics.jsonl`.

Condition names: `<agent>_<demonstration source>`; `Hu` = human recordings, `DfD` = generated by a trained DfD agent.
In folder names `default` is the DfD agent (its runs log to project `dfd_pusht`), and `AIdem` marks the
DfD-generated demonstration source.

## Demonstration sets

| task | human sets | machine sets | episodes per set | recorded |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PinPad-four | `HD_pinpad_four_0` … `_11` | `AD_pinpad_four_0` … `_11` | human 13–15, machine 15–20 | 2024-05-23 |

Shapes, read from the files: PushT episodes carry `pixels` and `image` at 94×94×3 uint8, `state` (5,), `action` (2,),
mean length 178 frames, about 17 800 frames per set. PinPad episodes carry `image` at 64×64×3 uint8, `action` (5,),
mean length 251 frames, about 3 300 frames per set.

Extra sets exist beyond the seeds used: PinPad has 12 of each where 8 are needed; PushT has an `HD_pusht_24` and
`AD_pusht_24` pair inside `old_or_incomplete`.

## Metrics: which key to use

| task | comparable across agents | not comparable |
|---|---|---|
| PinPad-four | `train_return` (both agents on the same scale, 0–50) | — |
| PushT | `original_reward` and `is_success` (0–1 for both agents) | `train_return`: IBC logs a per-episode sum reaching 704, DfD logs a 0–1 value |

Other keys present: `psuccess`, `eval_return`, `eval_length`, `eval_episodes`, and on PushT
`eval/original_reward`, `eval/is_success`, `eval/coverage`.
