## 1. What changed, and why (five things)

1. **One code tree, one reward ladder, no env-var gates.** {RLPD} and {r2dreamer} had trained on
   DIFFERENT ladders (`FULLENV_REWARD_X=1` was inert in the RLPD clone), and the (x) ladder's top rung
   could not be paid in training (the episode ended on an unpaid proxy). Both learners now import the
   same `baselines/rl/full_env.py` + `baselines/genesis_can_env.py` + `baselines/stage_predicates.py`;
   every run and every eval cell carries a provenance stamp (`[ladder] …`, `ladder_provenance.json`);
   launchers refuse legacy gates. Why: `paper/E2E_TRAINING_PROBLEMS_2026-09-10.md` §0,
   `paper/E2E_AUDIT_BRIEF_2026-09-10.md` §2/§8 (defects 1–5). Design: `paper/LADDER_UNIFY_BRIEF_2026-09-10.md`.
2. **`nested_proxy` is replaced by `nested_v2`** (released, at rest, within 0.081 m, both upright, not in
   hand; no gripper term, no settle simulation). The proxy's `contact` term was sticky and it reversed
   the human-vs-machine ordering. `nested_v2` = the settled predicate 60/60 on policy episodes.
   `paper/NESTED_V2_PREDICATE_2026-09-10.md`.
3. **The slide predicate is calibrated on the human tapes.** `slide_event` = released → gripper on
   the goal-opposite side within 0.10 m (60° cone) → goalward motion ≥ 1 cm counted only AFTER the
   released can has rested once (the set-down latch; the pilot's `slide_success` fired on set-down
   transients, `can_pos_recovery/videos_slide_smoke_2026-09-11/INDEX.md`). `home` = `slide_event` ∧
   `nested_v2`. On the demonstrations `home` fires on 13/13 human and 14/14 machine sim-slides and no
   other tape. `paper/LADDER_N_DEMO_CHECK_2026-09-11.md`; clips `can_pos_recovery/videos_home_2026-09-11/`.
4. **Tip rule:** threshold 60° and termination are RIGHT (0 of 25 tipped tapes recovers at any
   threshold; a free can passes its tipping point at ~33°); the guard was wrong (`grip < 0.3` fired
   with the can in hand on 6 demo tapes and missed 29 fist-flattened cans). New guard = tracker
   `not in_hand` sustained 4 frames, as an explicit stamped option. `paper/TIP_RULE_2026-09-11.md`.
5. **Demonstration sets locked down:** human `dHfull_all` (74 raw) v machine `dDPfull_first` (72,
   from a Diffusion-Policy teacher trained on the PRUNED full-task human set, first attempt per start);
   matched by phase within 0.06 under the new predicates. `HRI_results/DEMO_SETS_2026-09-11.md`;
   full reproduction chain `HRI_results/RESULTS_PROVENANCE_2026-09-11.md`.

Method change worth knowing: **stage records** — one termination-suppressed re-execution per tape
→ per-frame record → every ladder applied offline in numpy (`relabel_reward.py --from-records`;
offline == direct verified 146/146). Records of RECORD must be produced on the cluster's 64-core
class (this box disagrees on 6/74 + 8/72 tapes after contact).
