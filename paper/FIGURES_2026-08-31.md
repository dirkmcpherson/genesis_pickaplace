## fig6_tape_stats — demo-tape numerics, world × source (added 08-31 pm)
Built from `extract_tape_stats.py` (cluster, 228 tapes, images untouched) → `tape_stats.csv` +
`tape_paths.npz` (20-pt arc-length-resampled EEF paths), plotted by `make_fig6_tapes_2026-08-31.py`.
TOP ROW (per-tape geometry): wander, path length, tortuosity are FLAT across all four sets
(wander 0.061–0.063 m everywhere) — extends the 08-29 tape census (dH≈dDP within-world) across
worlds; the coarse "w3 human tapes are tighter" version of the coverage-erosion hypothesis is NOT
supported. BOTTOM ROW (set-level structure — the finding):
- (d) pooled EEF coverage (2 cm voxels): old dDP is the narrowest set (323 vs old dH 410, −21 %);
  in the corrected world the deficit nearly closes (dDP 440 vs dH 465, −5 %).
- (e) the world shift barely moves human tapes (same-IC path distance 0.017 m) but moves DP tapes
  3× more (0.054 m) — "dDP" is a different policy per world.
- (f) the DP teacher's tapes sit 2× closer to the human tapes in the corrected world (0.026 vs
  0.048 m same-IC) — the w3 teacher tracks its human data more faithfully.
READING: the coverage mechanism, measured on the tapes alone, PREDICTS the A20 interim pattern —
old-world machine demos are narrow (→ critic divergence, big source effect); corrected-world
machine demos are nearly human-broad (→ healthy dDP-RLPD, shrunken effect). CAVEATS: EEF-position
coverage only (not full state-action support); voxel counts depend on the 2 cm bin (relative
comparison only); resampled paths are 20 points (fine structure smoothed); A20 full-n scoring
pending — do not cite the mechanism-confirmation until it lands.

## fig7_real_vs_sim — the real command tapes vs what the sim training sets retain (added 08-31 pm)
Real side = FK of the recorded 60 Hz joint-position command streams through the same URDF the sim
uses (`extract_real_tape_stats.py`; COMMANDED kinematic path — the executed real path lives only
in the bags, task-3 machine). Sim side = the fig6 matched sets; same-trial pairing via ic_uid =
real trial uid (56 matched trials); paths start-aligned (absorbs the 3 cm mount offset).
FINDINGS:
- The real commanded signal is far richer than what survives re-execution: path length 1.83 m vs
  0.49/0.53 m (old/corrected), wander 0.095 vs 0.061 m, pooled coverage 731 voxels vs 374 (old)
  and 449 (corrected). The replay/execution stack is a heavy low-pass filter on the human data —
  and the CORRECTED world retains ~20 % more of the real coverage than the old world (449 vs 374
  on identical trials).
- Idle: 50 % of real frames command no joint movement (60 Hz frame level) — the raw material of
  the pruning story (matched dH sets are leading-idle-pruned → ~0).
- Same-trial shape distance real↔replay ≈ 0.19 m in BOTH worlds — and real↔DP-teacher is the
  same magnitude (0.185): the faithful "replay" of a human demo is no closer in path shape to the
  commanded signal than the DP teacher's independent rollout on that IC.
CAVEATS (load-bearing, fig7): the real path is commanded, not executed — 60 Hz command dithering
inflates its length/coverage relative to the smoothed executed sim tapes, so the multipliers
(3.5× path, 2× coverage) are upper bounds on "signal lost"; the qualitative ranking
(real > corrected > old retention) is the defensible claim. Same-trial distances are arc-length-
resampled SHAPE distances; the length mismatch itself contributes. Executed-real comparison
(bag tool_pose) is the task-3 machine's follow-up and would tighten all of this.

## fig8_wm_metric — what still separates human from machine tapes in the corrected world (added 09-01)
Built from `baselines/diagnostics/tape_dynamics_metrics.py` (cluster, same 228 matched tapes as
fig6, images untouched) → `tape_dyn_metrics.csv` + `set_level_metrics.csv`, plotted by
`make_fig8_wm_metric_2026-09-01.py`. SCREENING STUDY (38 metrics, unadjusted p; full honesty
protocol + ranking criterion in `paper/WM_METRIC_2026-09-01.md`). Panel (a) = criterion
C = Cohen's d(dH−dDP, w3) − d(old) for ALL 33 per-tape metrics; set-level coverage family inset.
FINDING: the w3-specific separators form one family — temporal burstiness (pause_frac 0.29 vs
0.17, d +1.35; act_hf_frac d +1.07; jerk_per_len sign-flips old→w3) — while every coverage/support
metric has NEGATIVE criterion (separates old world only; internal control = the fig6/A20 picture).
Hypothesis: the WM cares about excitation richness of (s,a)→s′ (stop-and-go transients), which
value-support RLPD does not need once state coverage is matched. Pre-registered ablation proposed
(WM_METRIC §5: burst-injection re-timing of dDP + de-paused dH + DART-noise control, r2dreamer w3).
CAVEATS: hypothesis-generating only; act_hf_frac's top rank partly inflated by old-world DP dither
(sign flip); dH = re-executed commands (burstiness gap is a lower bound vs raw commands).
