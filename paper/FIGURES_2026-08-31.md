## fig1_rlpd_rnd_violin — RLPD, the central result
LAST-checkpoint rnd-30 (the A16 pre-registered statistic) for six arms: success-only, +own-fails,
+duplicated-successes (row-matched control), each human vs DP-teacher. The +0.21 human gap holds in
success-only and +fails preparations; the dup controls agree directionally (+0.29, p 0.086, n=4 — n=8
queued 08-31). CAVEATS: sparse pair is n=7v7 (dH s30 hit the 12 h wall during its final eval; dDP s32
final eval missing — both disclosed in RESULTS §2); p-values are exact permutation, unadjusted across
the three brackets; dDPfails s32 (0.10) is the one diverged seed in that arm — it is IN the plot and
in the statistic.

## fig2_rlpd_divergence — the mechanism
Left: divergence rate per arm (max critic loss ≥ 1, A16). Right: per-seed max critic loss, log scale.
The two-panel story: DP-teacher successes destabilise the critic (6/8); adding the SAME teacher's
failure tapes cures it (1/8, losses drop two orders of magnitude); duplicated successes do not (2/4).
CAVEAT: divergence n=8 per fails/sparse arm, n=4 dup; threshold=1 was fixed in A16 BEFORE the s33-37
readouts; one human seed (s36, 4.42) also crossed it and still scored 25/30 — divergence is a
mechanism indicator, not a performance verdict (RESULTS §2.1).

## fig3_dp_violin — DP is (nearly) source-indifferent; pruning caveat
Left: corrected world n=10v10, hold and rnd, selected ckpt. The +0.06 rnd edge (p 0.041, unadjusted,
one comparison) is the only DP source effect. Right: old world — pruned human vs DP (n=5 each,
overlapping) and the UNPRUNED human set (stars, n=3 complete): −0.056 vs pruned, CI [−0.19, +0.08],
perm p 0.36 — no detectable penalty; the pruning step is efficiency, not load-bearing cleaning.
CAVEATS: unpruned set is N=52 vs 56 (9 ICs lack an unpruned success) and its tapes are ~14 % longer
in rows — the comparison is set-matched, not row-matched; n=3v5 is powered only for large effects
(min attainable perm p 0.036); old-world DP n=5 was never powered for a claim.

## fig4_r2d_w3 — world model, corrected world
BEST-checkpoint (highest in-job sel among archived ckpts) hold and rnd, n=4v4. Human ignites the WM
on 4/4 seeds; DP on 1/4 (s83 reaches 28/30 — the variance driver). CAVEATS: direction only (perm
p 0.143, min attainable 0.029 at n=4; s84-87 packs at ~1M/3M land ~09-01 → n=8v8); r2dreamer carries
the disclosed return-clamp deviation (RESULTS §3); BEST-of-K selection is doing real work — LAST-ckpt
scores are bistable (RESULTS §3.1); dense reward only (sparse never ignites).

## fig5_gap_forest — the paper in one figure
Human-minus-DP gap with Welch 95 % CI per learner/preparation, ordered IL → RL+demos → WM. The gap
grows as the learner leans harder on the demos; dv3 (unmodified port) is the footnote — no learner to
compare. CAVEATS: rows differ in world/checkpoint-rule as labelled (one world per table holds WITHIN
each learner, A19 — this cross-learner figure mixes worlds and says so); DP row unadjusted; the two
n=4 rows are in flight to n=8.
