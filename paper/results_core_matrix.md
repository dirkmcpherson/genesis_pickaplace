# Core matrix results (pick phase)

Numbers pulled fresh from wandb on 2026-08-09 (entity `jambotime`). Official
matrix numbers come from the `*-eval` runs in project `genesis_paper`
(keys `eval_indist/*`, `eval_random/*`, 15 episodes per eval); lineage numbers
from `genesis_pickaplace_ouro` (gen-0 provenance flagged below). All source
runs were created 2026-08-02 or later, i.e. after the 2026-07-30 eval-semantics
change — no pre-change numbers are used. **The seed wave landed 2026-08-08/09:

Metric shown is `picked` (pick-phase scope). `eval_indist` = demo-IC evaluation
(the ~3 demonstrated can positions; the headline). `eval_random` =
support-random ICs (generalization). The two are never mixed or averaged
together.

What it shows: model demonstrations beat human demonstrations in-distribution
at the pick phase (H1 inverted), and at n=8 the effect is no longer within
seed noise: the dDP_DP minimum (0.67) equals the dH_DP mean region's top
seeds, every dDP_DP seed is >= the dH_DP median (0.60), and the hierarchical
Bayesian analysis (paper/results_significance.md) puts
P(model > human) = 0.99 in-dist. On random ICs the sources are
indistinguishable (0.23 vs 0.23; P = 0.45) — the model-demo advantage is a
demo-IC phenomenon, not better generalization.

Caveats:
- n=8 seeds; ranges shown, 15 episodes per eval.
- The dH_DP numbers depend on idle-frame pruning (29.6% of frames dropped);
  the unpruned same-frames control sat at 0.27/0.13 — a ~2.5x preprocessing
  effect. Model harvests contain no idle frames by construction (closed-loop
  teachers), which is itself a property of the demo source, not a matched
  preprocessing step. A dDP in-dist win is partly "no idle frames by
  construction".
- Pick-phase scope only. Downstream metrics stay near zero for all DP seeds,
  with one asymmetry worth noting as context: dH_DP placed = 0.000 on every
  seed and both regimes, while dDP_DP reaches nonzero in-dist placed on all 8
  seeds (0.13–0.60, best `dDP_DP_s1-eval`); contact ≤ 0.07, nested 0
  everywhere. Descriptive context, not matched-protocol full-task results.

## SE-bar variants (`*_se.png`) and the range display

Each matrix figure has a standard-error variant: `figs/bc_central_picked_se.png`
and `figs/sacfd_picked_se.png` show mean ± SE = std(ddof=1)/sqrt(n) over seeds,
seeds still visible as dots. At n=8 the seed-level SE is meaningfully more
stable than it was at n=3, but the SACfD seed distribution is still
heavy-tailed (0.00–0.60), so the min/max-range versions remain the primary
display. For the lineage, `figs/lineage_picked_se.png` carries a different bar
entirely: with a SINGLE seed per generation, seed-SE is undefined, so its bars
are per-eval binomial SE sqrt(p(1-p)/15) over the 15 episodes — evaluation
noise, not seed variability. Do not compare lineage SE bars to the matrix SE
bars.

Computed SE values (seed-level, n=8; dDP_SACfD n=7): dH_DP in-dist
0.617±0.039, random 0.233±0.038; dDP_DP in-dist 0.800±0.038, random
0.225±0.028; dH_SACfD in-dist 0.200±0.075, random 0.142±0.046; dDP_SACfD
in-dist 0.286±0.068, random 0.133±0.036.

## Cross-learner reading (stated honestly)

BC favors model demonstrations in-distribution, and at n=8 this is now the
paper's one statistically resolved source effect: dDP_DP 0.80 (0.67–0.93) vs
dH_DP 0.62 (0.40–0.80) in-dist, hierarchical P(model > human) = 0.99
(paper/results_significance.md); on random ICs the sources tie (0.23 vs 0.23,
P = 0.45). SACfD shows the same direction in-dist (0.29 vs 0.20, P = 0.78)
but remains unresolved under its seed variance — and its n=3 ordering (human
ahead) flipped when the new seeds landed, which is the cautionary tale for
every small-n comparison in this paper. Together: no learner shows a
human-demo advantage anywhere; BC shows a decisive model-demo advantage on
demo ICs (with the idle-frame confound caveat attached), and neither learner
shows any source effect on random ICs — consistent with the generational
plateau (success-filtered teacher demos are clean imitation targets and
nothing collapses).

   The `ouro_dp_joint_dp_gen0` training run itself carries no eval summary.
   If a reviewer asks, the honest statement is: gen-0's eval is a same-recipe
   replicate, not a post-hoc eval of the very checkpoint that generated the
   gen-1 harvest. A direct eval of that checkpoint would close this gap.
