### Why 60 and not ≈72 (per-uid, no hidden drops)

Keep rate 60/72 = 0.83 ≈ the frozen sets' 0.85 (`dH_either` 56/66) and 0.88 (`dH_w2`
58/66). The A21 "expect ≈70+" was optimistic; every fail is one of the three documented
classes (all deterministic — the frozen 08-23 retry showed identical outcomes):

| class | old world | corrected world |
|---|---|---|
| lying-can IC, tip rule at t=0 (legacy lying-spawn placements; grip open + tilt → terminate n=1) | 234, 318 | 234, 318 |
| over-horizon: tape > 1200-sim-step contract cap (`env_truncated` at 300 decisions) | 245, 286, 293, 295, 299, 300, 331 | 245, 246, 286, 293, 295, 300, 331 |
| follower miss (`adapter_exhausted`, dilation 1.2–1.6; pick never hardens at repeat 4) | 233, 256 | 261, 278, 319 |
| tipped mid-follow | 246 (n=127) | — |

Notes: 233 (the user-validated "perfect" demo) nests at stride-1 replay but its r4
follower run misses in the old world only — it IS kept in the corrected world. 5 of the 6
newly enabled uids are kept in the old world (236/239/244/255/261; 331 is over-horizon in
both), 4 in the corrected (236/239/244/255).

## 8. Deviations / judgment calls (disclosed)

- Kept 60/72 per world, not the "≈70+" A21 hoped for — §3 itemizes all 24 fails into the
  three pre-existing failure classes; nothing new appeared, nothing was retried into the
  kept set (human follower is deterministic; the frozen block's retries proved it).
- Teacher trainings: **2 seeds per world** (4 jobs), mirroring the frozen pilot recipe
  (`dp_pilot` s0/s1, `dp_pilotw2` s0/s1) rather than the literal "one per world" — §3.1
  needs ≥2 seeds to apply its selection rule.
- Recording used `--arrival either` (the frozen block-of-record setting) — the ONLY
  intended difference vs the frozen recordings is the IC source (validated placements via
  reset(uid) instead of `--ic-from-tape`), plus the 6 extra uids.
- Corrected-world naming: `dHv2_w2` recording → `matched_w3/dHv2` (the existing `_w2` →
  `matched_w3` convention), not `dHv2@gc_kp4_riser3_shelf6`.
- 3118190's pax007 CUDA failure resubmitted as 3118203 with `--exclude=pax007`.
