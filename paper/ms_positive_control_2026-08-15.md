# RLPD positive control — our implementation on ManiSkill PickCube-v1

2026-08-15. Author: Fable (agent). Code: `baselines/rl/ms_env.py`,
`baselines/rl/train_rlpd_ms.py` (both new; nothing existing modified).

## 1. Why

Our RLPD arm ignites on the genesis pick task at a stable but low rate: four
independent waves, each with exactly one of three seeds at the ≥3/15 bar, pooled
5–7 picks per 45 eval episodes, and that signature was **invariant** to reward
density (25×), demo source, action encoding, and action repeat
(FABLE_HANDOFF_2026-08-13 §19–§21). The user's concern, verbatim:

> "the ignition rate / performance is so low that I'm not sure people will
> believe the RLPD results."

Two hypotheses survive: **(a)** our RLPD implementation has a defect, or **(b)**
the genesis task is simply that hard for RLfD at this budget. They are separated
by running *the same code* on *a published benchmark* with *published-quality
demos*. Pass ⇒ (a) is falsified and task difficulty becomes the paper's claim.
Fail ⇒ we have another bug, and finding it is worth more than the run costs.

This is a control on the **implementation**, so the machinery is frozen and only
horizon-scale parameters are re-tuned. Every such adaptation is listed in §4.

**Success predicate** (`--success-mode`, default `relaxed`):

* `relaxed` = `is_grasped ∧ is_obj_placed`. This is the dv3 MS wrapper's
  `custom_success_check` (grasped ∧ ‖obj→goal‖ < `goal_thresh`, **no** static
  requirement), which is why the control is comparable to the dv3 MS arm.
* `native` = ManiSkill's own `info['success']` = `is_obj_placed ∧ is_robot_static`.
  Published PickCube numbers use this one.

Both are computed and logged every step; only the selected one drives reward and
termination, **and the demo relabel uses the same one** (one predicate, two call
sites — the same discipline as `full_env.pick_hold_held`).

> **Caveat that must travel with every number from this file.** Under
> terminate-on-`relaxed`, the arm is mid-motion at the terminal step, so `native`
> success is mechanically rare there — measured on the demos themselves: only
> **11/50** motion-planned demos are `native`-successful at their `relaxed`
> terminal frame. `native` is therefore reported as a *diagnostic column*, not
> as the metric. A number from this control is not directly comparable to a
> published PickCube success rate that used the static requirement.

### Reward-semantics delta vs RLPD's Adroit

RLPD's Adroit tasks pay +1 on **every** solved step and run to the horizon (max
return ≫ 1). We pay +1 on the **first** solved step and terminate (max return
exactly 1.0). Terminating is what the dv3 MS runs and our genesis pick scope
both do, and holding the reward *shape* identical to the genesis arm is the
point of a control. Consequence: the Q-watchdog's `2.0` threshold (2× max task
return) is correct here **unchanged** — no rescale, unlike the hold-reward arm.

## 3. Demos — provenance and census

**Route chosen: (b′) ManiSkill's own shipped motion-planning demonstrations**,
read directly from
`~/.maniskill/demos/PickCube-v1/motionplanning/trajectory.state+rgb+depth.pd_ee_delta_pos.physx_cpu.h5`
(1000 episodes, 100% success, generated by MS's own motion-planning solver and
already replayed into `pd_ee_delta_pos` by MS's `replay_trajectory` tooling).
We take the first **50**.

Why not route (a), the local dv3 teleop npz: those *do* carry usable state
(`state (T,26)`, `privileged_state (T,42)`, `action (T,4)`) — the work order's
"they may be image-only" worry does not apply. Three reasons the h5 route wins,
and the teleop set stays available behind `--demo-source teleop`:

1. Only **10** episodes exist (~700 transitions after cutting at success).
   RLPD's Adroit setup uses 25 human demos; 50 MS demos give 3440 transitions.
2. Teleop lengths are 83–133 steps; three of ten **exceed the 100-step horizon**,
   i.e. the demonstrator is slower than the agent's own budget. Motion-planning
   demos are 49–92 steps, all inside it.
3. The h5 carries per-step `success`, `env_states` and the reset seed, which
   makes the fidelity gate in §5 possible; the npz does not.

**No replay is used to build the demo tensors.** The stored `obs/state` rows are
what this exact env config emitted when the file was recorded, and the actions
are already native `pd_ee_delta_pos`, so the tuples are on-MDP as they stand and
go straight into `DemoData(transitions, action_transform=None, …)`. Reward and
done are recomputed **from the stored observations** with the same predicate the
env uses, and each tape is cut at its first solved frame.

*Index-map self-check.* The 42-dim layout is derived at runtime from a live
`state_dict` env (never hardcoded), and every episode asserts that ManiSkill's
own recorded native-success flags are reproducible from the stored state. That
assert **fired** during development and caught a real error: `Panda.is_static`
uses `qvel[..., :-2]` (the two finger joints are excluded), not all 9 joints.

```
[demo census]
  source           : motionplanning (…pd_ee_delta_pos.physx_cpu.h5)
  success predicate: relaxed  (reward = +1 on it, then done)
  episodes         : 50 used / 50 requested (0 never solved -> dropped)
  raw lengths      : min 49 / med 76 / max 92 steps
  cut-at-success   : min 34 / med 70 / max 86 steps (0 exceed the 100-step horizon)
  transitions      : 3440
  rewarded frames  : 50  => DENSITY 1.453%
  expected rewarded demo frames per 128-sample demo half-batch: 1.86
  ManiSkill-native success also true at the cut frame: 11/50
```

Density in context: genesis terminal-only = **0.079%** (0.10 rewarded frames per
128-sample demo half-batch), genesis hold-reward = 1.96%, here **1.453%** (1.86
per half-batch) — percent-level, i.e. 18× the genesis default and squarely in
the regime every published RLPD setup operates in.

## 4. Ours vs theirs — parameter table

Machinery imported unmodified from `baselines/rl/rlpd_sac.py`. "Genesis arm" is
the `dH_RLPD-nb` configuration these results will be set against.

| parameter | RLPD paper (Ball 2023) | our genesis arm | this control | adaptation? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| critic ensemble E | 10 | 10 | **10** | none — the claim under test |
| target subset Z (min-of-Z) | 2 | 2 | **2** | none |
| critic LayerNorm | yes | yes (shared affine) | **yes (shared affine)** | none. Audit bug 2 (per-member LN) stays **off** so the control tests the *shipped* configuration; `--per-member-ln on` exists |
| critic depth | 3 hidden layers | 2 | **2** | none. Audit bug 3 carried deliberately — changing it would test a different implementation than the one that produced our rows |
| UTD | 20 (Adroit 20) | 10 | **10** | none |
| demo/online split | 50/50 | 50/50 (128+128 of 256) | **50/50 (128+128)** | none |
| BC loss | none | none | **none** | none |
| backup_entropy | **off** for every sparse domain | off | **off** | none |
| entropy coef | auto | auto | **auto** | none |
| target entropy | −dim/2 | −3.5 (7-dim) | **−2.0 (4-dim)** | *mechanical* — same −dim/2 rule, new action dim |
| actor loss | ensemble mean Q | ensemble mean Q | **ensemble mean Q** | none |
| lr / τ / batch / net | 3e-4 / 0.005 / 256 / (256,256) | same | **same** | none |
| **γ** | 0.99 (0.999 AntMaze) | 0.998 | **0.99** | **TASK-SCALE.** Genesis needs a ~500-step credit window (median pick frame 662); PickCube is solved in 34–86 steps, so 0.99 gives γ^100 ≈ 0.37 — visible. 0.99 is also RLPD's own published value outside AntMaze |
| buffer | 10^6 | 300k | **300k** | none — equals the step budget, so nothing is ever evicted |
| learning_starts | 5k (theirs, varies) | 1000 | **1000** | none |
| Q-watchdog | — | 2.0 (= 2× max return) | **2.0** | none — max return is 1.0 in both |
| episode horizon | domain-specific | 900 sim steps | **100 decision steps** | **TASK-SCALE** — the benchmark's own scale (dv3 MS used 100; stock MS 50) |
| env steps | 300k–1M | 100k | **300k** | **TASK-SCALE** budget, pre-registered below |

Net: **one** substantive hyperparameter changed (γ), one mechanical
(target_entropy = −dim/2 evaluated at dim 4), and two task-scale settings
(horizon, budget). The algorithm is byte-identical — `train_rlpd_ms.py` calls
`make_rlpd()` and `DemoData` from `rlpd_sac.py` with no monkey-patching.

## 6. PRE-REGISTERED BAR

> Registered 2026-08-15, **before any training run past the 600-step smoke** and
> before any eval beyond the step-0 negative control.
>
> **Our RLPD implementation is VALIDATED iff ≥2 of 3 seeds reach eval success
> ≥ 0.50 on PickCube-v1 by 300k env steps.**
>
> * eval = deterministic policy, n = 10 episodes, at every 50k-step checkpoint
>   (plus n = 20 at the end); success = the training predicate (`relaxed`), with
>   `native` success and grasp rate logged alongside as diagnostics.
> * "reach" = at any registered checkpoint ≤ 300k, not necessarily the last one
>   (post-ignition decay is a known RLPD failure mode and would be reported as
>   such, not hidden).
> * Basis: published RLPD-class methods solve sparse Adroit/PickCube-difficulty
>   tasks well inside 300k; ManiSkill's own PPO baseline solves PickCube quickly
>   at horizon 50; and dv3 solves this exact task from 10 demos by ~125k env
>   steps with a *pixels-only* policy — a state-space RLfD method with 50
>   solver-quality demos should not need more.
>
> **Outcomes.** Pass ⇒ hypothesis (a) is falsified; the machinery reproduces a
> published-class result and the genesis ignition rate becomes evidence about
> *task difficulty*, which is the paper's claim. Fail (0–1 seeds) ⇒ the defect
> is in our implementation, not in the genesis task, and the RLPD row must be
> reported as unvalidated until the bug is found. A 2/3 pass with wide variance
> is still a pass by the letter and will be reported with its variance, in the
> spirit of the letter/spirit distinction used for the hold and mref waves.

**Protocol caveats, stated up front.**

1. In-train eval, same process as training (acceptable here: unlike the genesis
   arm this is ManiSkill's own env with no cross-episode contamination history,
   and the P2 fresh-process discipline was a genesis-env finding). If a seed
   lands near the bar, the confirming number will be re-measured in a fresh
   process from the saved checkpoint.
2. The eval IC set is **fixed** (seeds 1e6 … 1e6+9, disjoint from training and
   from the demos), so checkpoints are compared on identical ICs. n = 10 gives a
   coarse grid: 0.5 has a 95% CI of roughly [0.19, 0.81].
3. Primary metric is the `relaxed` predicate (see §2 caveat) — not directly
   comparable to a published static-requirement PickCube number.
